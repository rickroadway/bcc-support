Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026. This policy describes the iPhone app.

BCC Business Command Center is provided by Richard Crawford. Privacy questions can be sent to rickroadway@gmail.com.

Your business records

BCC stores tasks, notes, business profile information, inventory, orders, imported marketplace reports and listing reviews in the app's private storage on your device. These records can include whatever personal or business information you choose to enter or import. BCC does not automatically send those records to the developer, connect to marketplace accounts or synchronize them with a developer-operated server. Core record-management workflows work offline and do not require a BCC account.

Exports, imports and backups

You choose files through the device's file picker and choose where exported reports and backups go through the system share sheet. A destination you select, including cloud storage or another app, may receive and retain that exported data under its own policies. Sharing is not automatic.

BCC's business-data folder is excluded from automatic device cloud backups. You are responsible for exporting and retaining backups. Unfinished capture drafts are stored locally but are not included in exported JSON backups. Local recovery copies created before an import or restore remain on the device unless you share them.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

BCC does not include an app advertising service, third-party behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking. Apple may separately process App Store, TestFlight, purchase or diagnostic information under Apple's privacy policies and your device settings. BCC's public App Privacy disclosures should be reviewed for each release if functionality or dependencies change.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message and attachments you send. Do not include passwords, signing keys or unnecessary customer information. Support correspondence is used to respond to the request and troubleshoot issues; the email provider also processes correspondence under its own policies. Contact support to request deletion of correspondence, subject to applicable record-retention obligations.

Removing information

Records remain locally until changed, replaced through restore or removed with the app. Deleting the app deletes its local workspace. BCC cannot recover local data without a backup you retain. Copies you previously exported, shared or saved with another service must be managed separately in that destination. The developer cannot remotely access or delete your device's business records.

Changes

This policy may be updated when BCC's functionality or data practices change. The published policy will show its effective date.
