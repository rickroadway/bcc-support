Support

Practical help for your local workspace.

Contact: rickroadway@gmail.com

For help, include your BCC version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected and the steps that produced the problem. Screenshots with customer and personal details removed are helpful. Do not send passwords or private keys. Send a business backup only if specifically requested and you are comfortable sharing its contents.

Keep a backup

Open Backup → Export backup and save the JSON through Files. For a device-only exported copy choose On My iPhone; a cloud destination you select may upload it. Keep an additional copy somewhere you control before replacing, deleting or reinstalling BCC. App deletion removes local records. Unfinished capture drafts are not included in JSON backups.

Where did an item go?

Clear Inventory/Orders search and filters. In Inbox, clear search and enable Show completed to find completed captures. Cancelled orders remain in history. Reserved and shipped quantities reduce available inventory; cancelled orders release their allocations.

Restore safely

Use Inspect backup without restoring to check a JSON backup first. Restore replaces saved business records and keeps a local pre-restore recovery ZIP. Your unfinished device-only capture draft remains separate. HTML and CSV exports are reports, not restorable BCC backups.

File imports and offline use

Marketplace imports use supported CSV files rather than connected marketplace accounts. Preview the file and confirm the channel before importing. Core work can continue offline; downloading an import file or sharing to a cloud destination may require connectivity. iCloud-hosted files may need downloading before they can be selected offline.

BCC has no BCC login, subscription or automatic sync in this release. App Store purchases and TestFlight access are managed by Apple.
