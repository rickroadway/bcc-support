A place for the work ahead.

Plan & capture

Set available time in Daily Brief. Save tasks and notes in Capture, then search, finish or reopen them in Inbox.

Stock & shipping

Track inventory stages, reserve stock for orders, record shipments and release allocations when an order is cancelled.

Back up & share

Export JSON backups through Files, inspect before restoring, and save HTML or CSV reports when you need a copy.

Keep a copy you control.

Business records stay in BCC’s private device storage until you export or share them. Deleting the app deletes local data, so export backups regularly. Unfinished capture drafts are not included in JSON backups.
